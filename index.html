About Me

Hi, I’m Willow Saan.

I’m an indie musician, songwriter, and mentor who believes that young artists do not need to wait until they are “ready” to start taking their music seriously. They need structure, feedback, confidence, and someone who understands the messy early stage of becoming an artist.

My music blends intimate lyrics, soft alternative sounds, and raw emotional storytelling. Through The Willow Train, I help emerging musicians sharpen their voice, develop their image, finish their songs, and learn how to share their work without watering themselves down.

This mentorship is for artists who are still finding themselves, but know they have something worth saying.

The Program

A mentorship built for artists in the messy beginning.

The Willow Train is a 12-week mentorship experience for young indie musicians who want practical help and creative direction. It is part coaching, part feedback lab, and part artist-development bootcamp.

Start Your Application

Learn how to turn scattered ideas, voice notes, and half-written lyrics into complete songs with emotional focus.

Clarify your sound, visuals, message, influences, and the feeling people should get when they hear your music.

Build stage presence, vocal confidence, and the ability to perform without hiding behind nervousness.

Plan a release rollout with content ideas, audience-building steps, and realistic goals for your next single or EP.

Services

Choose the support you need

Not every musician needs the same thing. Willow offers flexible mentorship options depending on your goals.

1:1 Mentorship

Weekly sessions focused on your songs, goals, artist identity, and creative blocks.

Song Reviews

Detailed feedback on lyrics, melody, structure, emotional impact, and overall direction.

Performance Coaching

Support with stage presence, confidence, live delivery, and connecting with an audience.

Release Planning

A clear plan for posting, promoting, and building momentum around your next release.
