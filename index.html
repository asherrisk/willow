About Willow Saan

Willow Saan is a prolific songwriter with a catalogue of over 400 original songs and performance experience across the Toronto area. A 2024 semi-finalist in a Canadian national songwriting competition, she blends dream-pop ballads with soulful downtempo country, defined by her signature piano style and sultry vocals. Her sound is both intimate and atmospheric, marked by emotional depth and refined musicality. Her music is streaming on all platforms. She also mentors young indie musicians and is one half of the emerging folk-rock duo Ellington Sun.

Services

Immerse yourself in the soul-stirring repertoire of Willow Saan, where each note is a heartfelt expression that transcends boundaries and connects with audiences on a profound level. Choose Willow for an unparalleled musical journey that will undoubtedly touch your heart and soul.

Live Performances

Willow enchants audiences with her heartfelt performances, crafting timeless ballads and upbeat tracks that resonate emotionally. She has performed extensively at a wide range of live events, including cafés, restaurants and bars, charity fundraisers for non-profit organizations, annual general meetings, and community festivals such as the Annual Caledon Day Festival.
